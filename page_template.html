How Moodio works
Emotion, lyric theme and occasion labels were written by an AI from what it knows of each song, so some are guesses. Lyrics are not copied. Songs play right here. Songs pulled from Spotify use Spotify's player (30-second previews, or full songs when you are signed in to Spotify). The other songs play a 30-second preview from iTunes.
